Install on iPhone

About 10 minutes, once.

  1. Install SideStore. Follow the official guide at sidestore.io. Recent versions install without a computer.
  2. Add Legacy Remote. In SideStore, open Sources, tap +, and paste this site's source.json address. Or open My Apps, tap +, and choose the file you downloaded.
  3. Allow it. Open Legacy Remote and tap Allow when it asks to find devices on your network.
  4. Keep it fresh. Apps from free Apple Accounts expire after 7 days. Open SideStore once a week to refresh it.
Install on Mac

Two minutes.

  1. Open the zip and drag Legacy Remote to Applications.
  2. Open it once. macOS will say it can't verify the developer. Click Done.
  3. Allow it. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Legacy Remote.
  4. Allow network access when asked, so it can reach your speakers and your iPhone.
