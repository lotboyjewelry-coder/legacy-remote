Why it exists

You paid for the speakers.
They should keep working.

The hardware still sounds great. Over the past six years, the software around it has let owners down.

  1. Oct 2019

    Working speakers, bricked for a discount

    Sonos's trade-up program required owners to put old speakers into Recycle Mode, which permanently disabled them after 21 days. Sonos dropped the requirement in March 2020 after the backlash.

    Engadget · Techdirt
  2. Jun 2020

    One home, two systems

    Sonos moved new products to S2 and left older ones on S1, with bug and security fixes only. Homes with both had to split into two systems that can't play together.

    What Hi-Fi? · Tech Advisor
  3. May 2024

    A new app, with features missing

    The redesigned app shipped without sleep timers, alarms, queue editing or local library browsing. Sonos later put the cost of fixing it at $20 to $30 million.

    "I want to begin by personally apologising for disappointing you."
    Sonos CEO, July 2024 · What Hi-Fi? · Galaxus
  4. Jan 2025

    The CEO steps down

    Patrick Spence left after eight years, following the widely criticized launch.

    PCWorld
  5. 2026

    The S1 app still stalls

    Owners report the S1 app hanging on a blank screen when they add a music service. The official workaround is to use a computer.

    Sonos Community
