Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026.

Legacy Remote does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The app connects only to the speakers on your own Wi-Fi network, to your own Mac if you use its music library, to radio stations you choose to play, and to the App Store to process the optional unlock. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details. Your room names and settings stay on your speakers and your device.

The app asks for Local Network access only to find and control your speakers.
